This project turned the mechanical energy of exercise into clean drinking water.

I designed and fabricated a belt-driven power-transmission system that stepped
cycling motion from $$\sim 45$$ to $$\sim 1000\,\text{rpm}$$ to drive a
reverse-osmosis booster diaphragm pump. The working prototype generated
$$\sim 60\,\text{psi}$$ and $$400\,\text{ml/min}$$ of purified water, reducing
total dissolved solids (TDS) from 350 to 25 ppm.
