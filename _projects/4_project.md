My Bachelor thesis designed and validated a shell-and-tube phase-change-material
(paraffin) thermal battery for energy storage.

I built a full CAD-to-mesh-to-CFD workflow (SolidWorks, HyperMesh, STAR-CCM+) to
model the transient charging process, optimizing PCM thickness, tube pitch, and
flow conditions. To validate the simulations, I designed and instrumented a
9-thermocouple PCM test rig, confirming the predicted melting trends across
multiple flow orientations and rates, and assessed nanoparticle-enhanced melting.
