During a summer internship at DRDO LASTEC, I designed a vapor-chamber plus
heat-sink assembly to air-cool an $$\sim 800\,\text{W}$$ dual-source heat load
from a high-power fiber laser, minimizing total thermal resistance across the
wall, vapor, wick, and fin.

I screened sintered-powder and grooved wick concepts by quantifying capillary
pressure limits against Darcy pressure drop, and optimized rectangular versus
pin-fin heat-sink geometries using boundary-layer and heat-transfer analysis.

A related winter project simulated helium flow through a Zanker flow conditioner
in ANSYS, evaluating flow stabilization, pressure drop, and development length
for a $$383\,\text{K}$$, 6 bar duct-flow application.
